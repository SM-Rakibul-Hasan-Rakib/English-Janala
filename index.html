Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I start learning English on this website?

You can start by exploring our beginner lessons, interactive exercises, and quizzes. We also offer structured courses to guide you step by step.

2. Is this website free to use?

Yes, most learning resources are available for free. Some premium features may require a subscription.

3. Do I need to create an account?

You can browse lessons without an account, but creating one helps track your progress.

4. How can I build my English vocabulary?

Practice daily with vocabulary exercises, flashcards, and reading activities available on the platform.

5. Do you offer certificates for completed courses?

Yes, certificates are available for selected courses after successful completion.
